# Information Security Policy

## Customer Information

Employees must not store customer Social Insurance Numbers (SIN) in 
unauthorized systems.

Customer information may only be stored in approved company platforms.

## Password Requirements

Passwords must contain:

- Minimum 12 characters
- Uppercase letters
- Lowercase letters
- Numbers
- Special characters

Passwords must be rotated every 90 days.

## Data Retention

Customer records must be retained for 7 years.

After the retention period, records must be securely deleted.

## Remote Access

Remote access is permitted only through company-approved VPN solutions.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required.

## Incident Reporting

Security incidents must be reported within 24 hours of discovery.
