# Regulatory Compliance Guidelines

## Personal Information

Personal information must be protected against unauthorized access, 
disclosure, or modification.

## Risk Levels

### Low Risk

Administrative policy questions.

### Medium Risk

Questions involving customer information.

### High Risk

Questions involving:

- Financial transactions
- Regulatory reporting
- Customer identification
- Privacy violations

## Audit Requirements

All compliance decisions must be logged.

The following information must be recorded:

- User
- Timestamp
- Question
- Response
- Supporting evidence
